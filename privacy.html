Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Nvaritia ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Nvaritia mobile application and website (nvaritia.online). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, chosen username, and password, along with optional profile details such as your bio and profile picture.
  • User-Generated Content: We store the posts, comments, reactions, direct messages, friend connections, and temporary 12-hour video stories you create or send on the platform.
  • Feed Personalization Data: To tailor your home feed, we process post view durations and interaction history.
  • Device Push Tokens: If you enable notifications on Android, we collect a device push token via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver alerts for direct messages, friend requests, story replies, and mentions.

2. Data Security & Retention

  • Encryption & Storage: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your account data and media are hosted securely on protected cloud servers.
  • Automatic Expiration: Stories automatically expire after 12 hours and are permanently deleted from our servers. Activity notifications older than 24 hours are automatically purged.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or private messages with third-party advertisers.

3. User Safety & Moderation

We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against abusive content. Users can report objectionable posts and block other accounts at any time. Blocking a user immediately removes their content from your feed, inbox, and connections.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time inside the app by going to Profile → Settings → Delete Account & Data. Deleting your account immediately and permanently erases your profile, posts, comments, direct messages, active stories, uploaded media, and notification tokens.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data deletion requests, contact us at: support@nvaritia.online
