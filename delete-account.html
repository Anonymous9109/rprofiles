Delete Your Nvaritia Account

You can permanently delete your Nvaritia account and all associated data either directly inside the Nvaritia app or by submitting the form below.

Option 1: Delete Inside the Nvaritia App

Open the app, log in, and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account & Data to immediately erase your account.

Option 2: Delete via Web Request

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account credentials, email, username, bio, and profile picture.
  • All posts, comments, reactions, active stories, and uploaded media files.
  • All direct messages, friend connections, follower lists, and push notification tokens.
